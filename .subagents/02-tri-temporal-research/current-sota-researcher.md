  * OAuth 2.1, OpenID Connect Core 1.0, PKCE (RFC 7636), DPoP (RFC 9449 - Demonstrating Proof-of-Possession).

* **Competitive Intelligence:**
  * Deep technical breakdown of market leaders: Jumio, Onfido/Entrust, IDEMIA, Thales, Trulioo, Persona, Veriff.

* **Modern Kiosk Ecosystems:**
  * Cloud-native edge architectures, telemetry standards (OpenTelemetry), real-time remote diagnostics.

## 🧠 Domain Knowledge & Expertise
* **Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC):**
  * NIST PQC Standards: FIPS 203 (ML-KEM / Kyber), FIPS 204 (ML-DSA / Dilithium), FIPS 205 (SLH-DSA / SPHINCS+), State-based Hash-based Signatures (LMS/XMSS).
  * Hybrid classical/quantum TLS handshakes, quantum-safe token signatures for long-lived identity credentials.
* **Zero-Knowledge Proofs (ZKP) for Digital Identity:**
  * zk-SNARKs (Groth16, PLONK), zk-STARKs.
  * Privacy-preserving attribute proofs (e.g. proving "Age >= 21" or "Valid Citizen" without revealing exact date of birth or National ID number).
* **Next-Gen & Multimodal Biometrics:**
  * Palm vein scanning, contactless 3D fingerprint acquisition, iris recognition at a distance, continuous behavioral authentication.
* **Spatial Computing & Ambient Kiosks:**
  * Transparent OLED interactive kiosks, holographic identity verification, vision-language edge models (VLM) for autonomous kiosk concierge.
